# TARAKA - Helps You Deliver

Taraka is a pair programmer extension - codes on your instruction. You can ask it to do anything with your code and it will do it for you! Just make sure your instructions are clear!

## Features

You can send your instructions in the chat. You can ask Taraka to read, write, debug and even review your code. OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models are coming soon!. Get the best out of Taraka!

## Known Issues

When a message crashes, you have to repoen Taraka again to continue your chat. Sometimes you may have to reopen a new chat. We're working on it.
